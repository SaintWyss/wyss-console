<engine_spec>
<!-- Esqueleto del engine — RESEARCH §Pattern 3 y §"Política: evaluación del plan completo" (líneas 553-580). -->

- `CognitivePolicyEngine.__init__(self, rules: PolicyRules)`.
- `evaluate(self, plan: Plan) -> PolicyDecision`:
  1. `deny_hits`: por cada `rule` en `self._rules.deny`, hit si `any(_matches(rule, a) for a in plan.steps)` o `_matches_aggregate(rule, plan)`. Si hay hits → `PolicyDecision(risk_level=D, outcome=REJECT, matched_rules=deny_hits, reasons=...)` incondicional.
  2. `per_action = max((_base_level(a) for a in plan.steps), default=RiskLevel.A)`.
  3. `aggregate = _aggregate_level(plan)` (POL-05, plan entero).
  4. `final = max(per_action, aggregate, plan.estimated_risk or RiskLevel.A)` (POL-04: estimated solo sube).
  5. `outcome = outcome_for(final)`.
- `_base_level(action)`: `self._rules.base_levels.get(action.type, self._rules.unknown_action_level)` — desconocido → C (fail-closed). NO exponer un `evaluate_action()` público (invita al bypass).
- `_matches(rule, action)`: `fnmatch(action.type, rule.action_type)` AND (rule.target_patterns vacío OR `any(fnmatch(action.target_normalizado, p))`) AND (`rule.params_equal` ⊆ `action.parameters`).
- `_matches_aggregate(rule, plan)`: para reglas tipo `mass-delete` con umbral agregado (ej. count(file.delete) > thresholds.delete_count_d → hit de la regla mass-delete).
- `_aggregate_level(plan)`: cuenta targets distintos, dirs distintos tocados por escritura, deletes; aplica los umbrales de `thresholds` (>file_count_b→B, >file_count_c→C, >dir_count_c→C, cualquier file.delete→mínimo C per docs/13, target bajo sensitive_dir en write→mínimo C). Devuelve el RiskLevel agregado.
</engine_spec>
