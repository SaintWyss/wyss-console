# 26 — Readiness assessment

How far the design is, on 2026-10-13, after the independent review, the corrections that
followed it, and the reconciliation with the owner's decision that a protected private backup
is allowed. Written by the author; the independent reviewer's own labels are in
[25 §2](25-INDEPENDENT-REVIEW.md) and are stricter in three places, shown below.

**The architecture is not complete, and it is not accepted.** Thirty documents and two proposed
ADRs exist; that is not the measure. The measure is whether a developer could build each part
without inventing a decision that belongs to the owner or assuming a provider behaviour nobody
observed. By that measure: one part of the new work is designed and unverified; the central
feature now has a designed path, with the owner's direction given on its open questions (2026-10-13); it waits for an independent review and for his formal acceptance, no longer for
research on a real provider; and seven domains of the final product lack design depth
([29 §5](29-CAPABILITY-PRESERVATION.md)). A coverage audit of the whole final product, with a
weighted documentation figure and its method, is [31](31-PLANNING-COVERAGE-AUDIT.md): about half
of the final product is planned to the depth a builder needs, and none of what was written after
the first rejection is reviewed.

Two independent reviews returned **REJECT**: the first of the whole set (2026-10-12), the second, focused on the vault and the write protocol (2026-10-13; 25 §8). Everything written since each, the corrections of the second included, has been read by its author only.

Implementation is not authorised for any part, whatever its label here (22, DN-16). Experiments
with provider binaries are not authorised either (22, DN-24).

## 1. Labels

| Label                         | Means                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| COMPLETE                      | Designed, and either built and tested `[R]` or with nothing left to decide or find out                                                           |
| COMPLETE BUT UNVERIFIED       | Designed to the level of interfaces, states, failure behaviour and tests; rests on provider behaviour or numbers nobody has observed or measured |
| PARTIALLY DESIGNED            | Requirements and options exist; interfaces, states, threat model or tests do not                                                                 |
| BLOCKED BY OWNER DECISION     | Cannot be finished on paper until a named decision is taken                                                                                      |
| BLOCKED BY TECHNICAL EVIDENCE | Cannot be finished on paper until a named observation or measurement exists                                                                      |

A part can carry two blocks. The design of a blocked part may be detailed; the label says that
detail cannot be relied on yet. No label here means "reviewed": see §5.
