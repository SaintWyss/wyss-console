### 8.2 The owner's guidance after the corrections

On 2026-10-13, having read the report of the corrections, the owner let them stand in the
proposed documents, said that the two REJECT verdicts are not overturned and that no guarantee
is validated, and gave nine points of guidance (listed in ADR 0029). Two of them changed what
§8 had corrected:

| Guidance                                                                                                                                                                                              | What it changed                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Do not couple read-only operations or verified provider-native switches to the vault without need; a locked keyring must not disable the Console                                                      | The correction of F-05 had made the vault a precondition of every direct write and said no more. A table now states, capability by capability, what each state of the vault stops and what it does not (27 §5.7)                                                                                                           |
| No indefinite conflict preservation in clear merely because the directory is private; prepare recovery material before the mutation where possible; model failures after it without claiming recovery | The correction of F-05 had left a version in clear "until it can be sealed". Sealing is now prepared before the write and needed in one rare case only. The author then proposed to bound the hold and destroy the version at a limit; **the owner rejected that the same day** (§8.3), and the documents no longer say it |

Two more paths that ended in sealing were found not to need it and now end in the ordinary
rollback: a process that still holds the old file at the ceiling, and an old file found
changed at start. Neither was asked for by the reviewer; both reduce how often a version has
to be preserved at all.
